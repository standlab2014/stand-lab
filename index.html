Hanna Cho LAB

We seek to read Alzheimer's disease in the living brain, in the two decades before dementia appears. Our laboratory combines tau and amyloid PET imaging with blood-based biomarkers to determine who is on the path, how quickly they are moving along it, and what can still be changed.
