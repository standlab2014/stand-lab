About us

A compass
for dementia

The lab is based at the Future Medical Research Center of Gangnam Severance Hospital, in the Department of Neurology at Yonsei University College of Medicine.

It grew out of a memory clinic — out of the gap between what a scan could show and what a patient could be told. That gap is closing. Tau PET can now stage the disease in a living person, and a blood test can flag the same pathology without a scanner. Our work is to make those readings dependable enough to act on.

Principal investigator

Hanna Cho, M.D., Ph.D.

Professor of Neurology

Hanna Cho is Professor of Neurology at Gangnam Severance Hospital, Yonsei University College of Medicine, where she leads the Seoul AD Biomarker Team. She trained in medicine at Yonsei University and completed her M.S. and Ph.D. at its Graduate School of Medicine, followed by a neurology fellowship at Samsung Medical Center. She was a visiting scholar at the UCSF Memory and Aging Center and studied Equity in Brain Health at the Global Brain Health Institute, UCSF.

Her research centres on tau and amyloid PET imaging and blood-based biomarkers of Alzheimer's disease. She serves as Director of Research for the Korean Dementia Association and sits on the Scientific Program Committee of the Alzheimer's Association International Conference.
